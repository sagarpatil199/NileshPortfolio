About

Where magnetism meets energy storage

Dr. Nilesh Chougle is an experimental physicist working at the interface of magnetism and electrochemistry. His research asks a simple but under-explored question: what happens to a supercapacitor electrode when you switch on a magnetic field?

His work spans the synthesis of perovskite orthoferrites (LaFeO₃, NdFeO₃), lead-based multiferroics such as Pb(Fe₀.₆₇W₀.₃₃)O₃, rare-earth doped spinel ferrites and doped hematite nanosheets — characterised through XRD, Raman, magnetometry, Mössbauer spectroscopy and full electrochemical analysis (CV, GCD, EIS) with and without applied fields.

He collaborates with groups in India, Taiwan, Brazil and Russia, and has co-authored work in the Journal of Energy Storage, Journal of Power Sources and Journal of Alloys and Compounds.

Research themes

Three connected directions

Magneto-electrochemical energy storage

Probing how mT-scale magnetic fields alter specific capacitance, ion diffusion and charge-transfer kinetics in oxide supercapacitor electrodes.

Multiferroics & magnetocalorics

Room-temperature magnetoelectric coupling, spin-glass behaviour, memory effects and magnetic entropy change in Pb-based perovskites and chalcogenide alloys.
